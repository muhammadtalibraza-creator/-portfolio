About Me

A concise snapshot of who I am, what I do, and where I’m headed.

I am an Environmental Engineering graduate from the University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Taxila with a strong foundation in environmental management, wastewater and solid waste management, HSE practices, environmental regulations, and sustainability. I am familiar with NEQS standards, industrial effluent management, and environmental compliance.

My undergraduate research focused on optimizing a coagulation–flocculation and chemical oxidation process for beverage industrial effluent, achieving final COD and BOD₅ concentrations of 115 mg/L and 68 mg/L respectively — meeting national discharge standards as verified by an accredited laboratory.

I have practical exposure to water and wastewater analysis (COD, BOD₅, solids, turbidity, jar testing), GIS mapping, EPANET, and Life Cycle Assessment (ISO 14040/14044). I have contributed to multiple accepted conference papers on wastewater sustainability, environmental risk assessment, and cleaner production.

I am eager to apply technical and analytical skills to support safe, sustainable, and environmentally responsible operations across industries, consultancies, utilities, and research organizations.

Process Optimization for Effective BOD & COD Removal in Beverage Industrial Effluent

Sep 2025 – Jun 2026 · Final Year Project · UET Taxila

Objective: Reduce BOD and COD in beverage industry wastewater using physicochemical treatment.

Problem: After anaerobic treatment, effluent still contained 980 mg/L COD and 427 mg/L BOD₅ — well above discharge limits.

Approach: Optimized pH and coagulant dosing, followed by oxidative polishing.
